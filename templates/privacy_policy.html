PRIVACY POLICY

Effective date: 27 September 2026

Gym Workout Generator ("this site") is a free web application that creates personalised gym workout plans. This policy explains, in plain English, what information the site collects and what it does with it. We keep it short because the honest answer is: we collect very little.

What we collect

Account information. If you create an account, we store the username you choose and your password. Your password is never stored in plain text — it is hashed (PBKDF2-SHA256 with a unique salt) before it is saved.

Your saved workouts. If you save a workout, we store the workout itself (your goal, days per week, experience level, session length and exercises), the date you saved it, and which account it belongs to.

Technical data. The site uses a single essential cookie so you can stay logged in, and it temporarily sees your IP address in order to rate-limit requests and prevent abuse. Your theme preference (dark/light) is stored in your own browser and never sent to us.

What we don't collect

We do not ask for or store your email address, real name, or any payment details. The numbers you enter into the calorie calculator (weight, height, age, sex) are sent only to calculate your result — they are never stored.

We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers of any kind, and we do not share, sell, or transfer your data to anyone.

How your data is used

Solely to operate the site: to log you in, to show you your saved workouts, and to protect the service from abuse.

How long we keep it

Until you delete it. You can delete individual saved workouts at any time, or delete your account from the settings menu — deleting your account permanently removes your username, your password, and every workout you have saved.

Security

Passwords are hashed, forms are CSRF-protected, and requests are rate-limited. No system is perfectly secure, so we deliberately store the minimum amount of data necessary.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
